# Plugin marketplace

::: info Under construction
The plugin marketplace is part of Blue's official-release roadmap and is not open during the preview stage. This page will be updated as the market opens.
:::

## What will live here

- **Discover** — browse community plugins for Blue: status-bar entries, tool cards, editor enhancements, whole new themes, complete panes;
- **Install** — one `dsh plugin add` line into your profile, and one line to remove — every Blue surface is a patch row, and the marketplace just makes "add a row" easy;
- **Publish** — your plugin is a Cordis plugin + a subpath export (see [Writing a Blue plugin](/en/plugins/)); publish to npm and it can be listed.

## Until the market opens

- The built-in enhancements (bash mode, image paste, git status, the todo pane, …) all ship with Blue itself — see the [features overview](/en/features/);
- To integrate your own plugin today, read [Writing a Blue plugin](/en/plugins/) — the seams are already open;
- Watch the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/dsh-blue/blue) for the marketplace announcement.
